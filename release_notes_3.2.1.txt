# Schoolbooth v3.2.1

## Bug fix: countdown skipped on subsequent multi-shot captures

In v3.2.0 Multi Shot mode, only the first photo of a session triggered the
on-screen countdown -- every following capture in the same session fired
instantly with no countdown. Root cause: `capture_image` treated the
previous photo's "review" overlay on the secondary display as a busy
state and skipped the countdown.

Fix: pressing CAPTURE while the previous photo is still being reviewed
now ends the review immediately and starts a fresh countdown over the
live feed. Pressing CAPTURE during an active countdown or shutter flash
is still ignored (unchanged).

No behavior change in Single Shot mode.
